# Sport-specific research

Read the section for the selected game. Exact-game rules override these decision considerations.

## Football

Verify round membership and the final round. Moved fixtures can change a fantasy round; real league season length does not establish the game's horizon.

Research likely minutes, starting roles, set pieces, penalties, injuries and expected returns, competition-specific suspensions, European rotation, and departures. A short absence may be acceptable for a fixed season team. Distinguish transfers within the league from departure/deactivation.

Use the game's listed positions for scoring, including real attackers registered as midfielders. Check clean-sheet participation thresholds and whether team-goal rewards depend on being on the pitch. Compare full-horizon fixtures for fixed teams and nearer fixtures for managed teams. With scarce contracts, prioritize persistent problems over marginal form switches.

## Cycling

Verify route, stage order, start list, and scoring. Research official roles, GC/jersey ambitions, sprint opportunities, time trials, breakaway freedom, health, and finishing likelihood. Another race's historical growth is comparative evidence, not a transferable scoring model.

For active teams, compare next-stage gains with fees and the cost of changing back. For limited changes, plan feasible sequences with a reserve and conditional future affordability. Fixed riders need whole-route evaluation: daily GC/jersey growth, time-loss deductions, teammate bonuses, and individual opportunities. Cheap domestiques are not automatically value.

Model a fixed captain across the whole horizon, including verified positive-only bonuses. Do not use an unrequested captain rotation. Portfolio identities can express GC depth, star concentration, or breakaways without forcing zero overlap.

## Formula 1

Verify driver/constructor/pitcrew quotas and captain eligibility. Different entities have distinct scoring paths: driver qualifying/results, combined cars for constructors, completion/points for pitcrews. Track correlated real-team exposure.

Research pace, reliability, upgrades, circuit suitability, weather, penalties, driver changes, and sprint weekends. Verify locks against qualifying or sprint qualifying rather than the race start.

Fixed teams need a remaining-calendar case. Compare star-driver concentration with constructor/pitcrew investment. Gold does not imply unlimited changes; exact contract limits and fees govern.

## Golf

Establish points versus money, category quotas, tournament classes, captain bonuses, and how one or several tournaments map to a Holdet round. Do not apply a money budget to a category/points game.

Research entry lists, intended schedules, eligibility, ranking qualification, cuts, withdrawals, course suitability, and remaining event classes. Separate confirmed starts from expectations. Participation may outweigh star quality across a fixed horizon. Historical totals do not establish future entry frequency.

A fixed captain must be evaluated for his actual remaining starts. Do not select a substitute captain silently when he is absent. Portfolio identities should express substantive participation, course, or upside assumptions.

## NFL

Use the game's positional requirements and scoring, not another platform's rules. Research depth charts, expected snaps/touches/targets, injury duration, roster cuts, transactions, bye weeks, schedule, and the actual fantasy horizon.

For fixed teams account for byes and persistent role risks. A permanent captain needs a full-horizon case. Distinguish confirmed moves and Holdet deactivation from speculative transactions.

Assess correlation in quarterback/receiver or club-focused teams. Keep the strongest overall team separate from speculative scenarios and explain each scenario's downside.
